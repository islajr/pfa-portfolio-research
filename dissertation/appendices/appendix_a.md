Dear Investment Professional,

You are invited to participate in a research study examining how Pension Fund Administrators approach the selection of direct real estate investments and the decision-making processes that shape property portfolio construction.

The study forms part of an undergraduate dissertation in Estate Management at Obafemi Awolowo University. It seeks to understand the factors and reasoning frameworks applied by experienced investment professionals when evaluating and selecting properties for institutional portfolios.

There are no right or wrong answers. Your responses are valuable precisely because they reflect your professional experience and judgment.

All responses are strictly confidential. Data will be reported only in aggregate form. No individual respondent, fund, or organisation will be identified in any publication or communication arising from this research.
